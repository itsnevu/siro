The nomination remains pending until the nominated wallet calls acceptOwnership() on each contract. The wallet already holds the owner role; the deployment did not transfer control to a different address.

The vault was deployed with a 25 bps creation fee. Its feeRouter() recipient is that same administrator address, rather than a separately deployed FeeRouter contract. Fees can change within the 50 bps cap after a 48-hour delay; forms read the current fee before a deposit. Deployment does not establish an independent security audit or explorer source verification.
